Our Story

History & Legacy

Rajini Kamal Films International (RKFI) is an Indian film production and distribution company founded and headed by Haasan Brothers. Raja Paarvai (1981) was the first film to be produced by them under the banner "Haasan Brothers" before renaming it to Raaj Kamal Films International.

The company was established by legendary actor-filmmaker Kamal Haasan and is headquartered in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. Over four decades, RKFI has produced and distributed over 30 feature films spanning Tamil, Hindi, and Telugu languages. Many of these productions have starred Kamal Haasan himself in lead roles, showcasing his versatility as an actor, writer, and director.

RKFI is widely recognized for pushing the boundaries of Indian cinema — pioneering new genres, experimental storytelling techniques, and cutting-edge visual effects. The banner has been behind some of the most critically acclaimed and commercially successful Indian films, many of which have represented India at international film festivals and at the Academy Awards.

Recognition

Awards & Achievements

RKFI productions have earned widespread acclaim at national and international forums, reflecting the company's commitment to cinematic excellence.

🏅

National Film Awards

Thevar Magan (1992) won the prestigious National Award for Best Tamil Film. Multiple RKFI productions have received National Film Award nominations across categories including Best Actor, Best Director, and Best Screenplay.

🎭

India's Official Oscar Entries

Three RKFI films were selected as India's official entries for the Academy Awards (Oscars): Thevar Magan (1993), Kuruthipunal (1996), and Hey Ram (2000) — a rare distinction for any single production house.

🌐

International Film Festivals

Virumaandi (2004) won the Best Asian Film at the Puchon International Fantastic Film Festival, South Korea. Uttama Villain (2015) won Best Film at the Los Angeles Independent Film Festival.

🏆

Cinema Express Awards

Kuruthipunal won the Cinema Express Award for Best Film – Tamil. The film, a gripping anti-narcotics thriller, was praised for its realism and powerful performances by Kamal Haasan and Arjun.

⭐

Filmfare Awards

RKFI productions have garnered multiple Filmfare Awards and Filmfare Awards South, including wins for Best Film, Best Actor, and Best Director across various editions recognizing both Tamil and Hindi releases.

🎥

Box Office Milestones

Vikram (2022) became one of the highest-grossing Tamil films of all time, crossing ₹400 crores worldwide. Dasavathaaram (2008) and Vishwaroopam (2013) were also major blockbusters.
